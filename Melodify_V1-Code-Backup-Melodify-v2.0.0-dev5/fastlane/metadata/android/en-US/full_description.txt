Melodify is a modern, privacy-friendly music and offline player for Android, designed for seamless high-fidelity audio playback.

Key Features:
- Ad-free Music Streaming: Enjoy your favorite songs and discover trending tracks with zero commercial interruptions.
- Offline Playback: Play your local audio library seamlessly anywhere, anytime without an internet connection.
- Real-Time Synced Lyrics: Sing along with word-by-word, beautiful karaoke-style synchronized lyrics.
- Smart Library: Save your favorite songs, create custom playlists, and access your listening history.
- Background & Lock Screen Controls: Full notification and lock screen playback controls with rich album art.
- Audio Engine & Haptic Bass: Fine-tune your listening experience with equalizer presets and tactile haptic feedback.
- Clean & Elegant UI: AMOLED black theme with fluid micro-animations and zero clutter.

Melodify is free, open source, and respects your privacy.
